# Feature: products/management (PROD)

## Overview

Creating, editing and deleting products and uploading their images: pages `/products/add` and `/products/[id]/edit`,
delete from the list card with a 5-second undo; `POST /products/add`, `PATCH /products/:id`, `DELETE /products/:id`,
`POST /uploads`. Used by every visitor (the app has no login).
